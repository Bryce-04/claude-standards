# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability
Do not open a public issue. Report it privately to <security contact email> or through the "Report a vulnerability" button on the repository's Security tab.

Include what you found, steps to reproduce, and the impact. You will get a reply within <N> business days.

## Supported versions
| Version | Supported |
|---------|-----------|
| <latest> | Yes |
| older | No |

## Handling
Reports are acknowledged, triaged, fixed in private, and disclosed with a release note once a fix is available.
